### Creation process
Originally the idea was to create a set of concentric rings angled apart such that the marble falls from one ring to the other, like in the preview below, but due to the mechanism required to spin the marbles along the tracks they couldnt fit together and stay concentric.

<img width="300" height="300" alt="OldPreview" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/53bbcf04-3e39-4710-ba06-33fb1a50c472" />

Then, due to realizing the idea was both a mechanical nightmare and some parts couldnt fit together, I pivoted to this idea with the arms:
<img width="800" height="450" alt="Preview" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ad826b9e-27e9-4796-8b94-f34761338106" />

After designing the main arms and testing feasability in blender with rigidbody simulations, I started the modeling in Fusion where my main focus was printability and ease of assembly. All of the parts are designed to either be snapped together or glued together.
<img width="822" height="729" alt="Untitled" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d69b77ea-014b-4e9c-af23-3f22e333737b" />
<img width="1101" height="622" alt="Untitled" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/caf37cd5-54c7-46d1-b83e-414fb98095d7" />
